What just happened

    Three experiments

    1. Crash the leader. Press "Stop the current leader". A follower runs out of patience, asks for votes, and a new leader takes over in a higher term. No committed write is lost.
    2. Lose the majority. Stop three servers. The two left can never win an election, so writes stop being committed. The cluster chooses to be unavailable instead of wrong.
    3. Split brain. Select the leader and cut it off the network. Keep it selected and send two writes: it accepts them but can never commit them (dashed). Meanwhile the other four elect a new leader. Reconnect the old one: it steps down at once, and on the next client write it throws away its dashed entries to match the new leader.
